## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Personal Finance helps you stay on top of your money without the clutter.

Set your starting balance for the month, log your expenses as they happen, and always know exactly how much you have left. No budgeting jargon, no complicated categories to set up — just a clear, honest view of your spending.

**Key features:**

- **Monthly balance tracking** — Set a starting balance (your income or budget) for each month and watch your current balance update automatically as you log expenses.
- **Simple expense entry** — Just the amount, a description, and the date. No unnecessary steps.
- **Recurring expenses** — Mark a bill as Fixed (repeats every month, like rent or a subscription) or Installments (repeats for a set number of months, like a payment plan), and it's handled automatically — no need to re-enter it each month.
- **Never miss a due date** — Expenses that are past due and unpaid are highlighted in red, with a running tally shown at the top so you always know what needs attention.
- **Mark bills as paid** — Track what's been settled with a clear, color-coded view of what's paid and what's overdue.
- **Duplicate with one tap** — Quickly copy an expense to next month instead of retyping it.
- **Your data, everywhere** — Log in with your account on any device and your data is right there, securely synced.

Personal Finance is built for anyone who wants a straightforward way to answer one question: "How much money do I actually have left this month?"
