Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Personal Finance ("the App") is a personal expense tracking app. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects and how it is used.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: your email address and a securely hashed password, used to create and log into your account.
  • Financial data you enter: expenses, amounts, descriptions, dates, categories, and starting balances you manually add to the App.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or any data from your device beyond what you type into the App.

How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely to provide the App's core functionality: storing and displaying your expenses and balances back to you when you log in, on any device.

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers (hosted via Render and Neon, both reputable third-party infrastructure providers) and is associated only with your account. Passwords are never stored in plain text.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal or financial data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes. Your data is never shared with other users of the App.

Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
