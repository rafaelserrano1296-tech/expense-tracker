Delete Your Account and Data

App: Personal Finance (developer: Rafael Serrano)

How to request deletion

To delete your Personal Finance account and all associated data, send an email request:

  1. Send an email to rafael.serrano1296@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject line: "Delete my Personal Finance account"
  3. Include the email address you used to sign up for the app

We will process your request and confirm by email once it's complete, typically within 7 business days.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your account (email and password)
  • All expenses, categories, and starting balances you entered

This data cannot be recovered once deleted.

Retention period

Data is deleted from our active database within 7 business days of a verified request. Backups that may contain your data are rotated out and fully purged within 30 days.

Questions

Contact rafael.serrano1296@gmail.com with any questions about this process.
